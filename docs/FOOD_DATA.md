# Food data (v0.1)

Every vanilla Vintage Story 1.22.7 food, its vanilla satiety, and the real-world nutrients Complex Nutrition gives it. Generated by `tools/build_food_profiles.py`; do not edit by hand.

**How portions are sized.** One in-game portion is set so its calories equal its vanilla satiety (1.0 kcal per satiety point). Vanilla drains about 0.96 satiety per real second while active and a quarter of that while idle, which at default speed is roughly 700 to 2,800 satiety per in-game day, close to a real adult's 2,000 to 2,800 kcal. Keeping kcal equal to satiety leaves vanilla's food balance untouched; the nutrients then follow from the real food. Low-calorie foods therefore get large portions (a whole cabbage head is about 1.2 kg).

**Sources.** USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018). Micronutrients are % of the US FDA Daily Value; "B" is the mean %DV of thiamin, riboflavin, niacin, B6, folate and B12 (each capped at 300%). Liquids follow the same rule per in-game litre, so "1 L = 139 g real" means an in-game litre of milk carries the nutrients of 139 g of real milk. Where USDA has no matching food, the nearest real food is used and the note says so.
