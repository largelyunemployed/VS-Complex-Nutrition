# Food data (v0.1)

Every vanilla Vintage Story 1.22.7 food, its vanilla satiety, and the real-world nutrients Complex Nutrition gives it. Generated by `tools/build_food_profiles.py`; do not edit by hand.

**How portions are sized.** Each item has a real-world weight chosen for what it looks like in game (an apple is 180 g, a cabbage head 900 g, a loaf 225 g, a cut of raw red meat 300 g). Calories and nutrients follow from that weight, so they are no longer tied to vanilla satiety; the Satiety and kcal columns show how the two compare. Liquids are given per litre. Only foods with no profile of their own (the insects below, and foods from other mods) are sized from satiety, at 1.0 kcal per satiety point, because nothing else is known about them.

**Sources.** USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018). Micronutrients are % of the US FDA Daily Value; "B" is the mean %DV of thiamin, riboflavin, niacin, B6, folate and B12 (each capped at 300%). Where USDA has no matching food, the nearest real food is used and the note says so.
