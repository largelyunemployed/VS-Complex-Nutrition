**⚠️ This mod's code was written with AI assistance (Anthropic's Claude), under human direction and review.**

**Complex Nutrition** makes what you eat matter. Every food in vanilla Vintage Story gets real-world calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber, vitamins and minerals. Your body keeps reserves of each one, and the way you eat over weeks of in-game time decides how you feel.

- **Eat well, thrive:** a balanced diet raises healing, max health and movement speed, but your body burns more calories.
- **Eat narrowly, suffer slowly:** living on one food group wears you down, and severe malnutrition hurts.
- **Everything is optional:** each part of the mod has its own switch, so you can keep what you like and turn off the rest.

Requires Vintage Story 1.22.7. Status: in development.
